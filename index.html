FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required for a salaried personal loan?
Generally, you need your latest 3 months' salary slips, 6 months' bank statements, PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, and proof of address.
Are there any upfront fees for checking loan eligibility?
No, checking your eligibility with Star Bridge Finance is 100% free with zero hidden charges.
How long does the loan approval process take?
Once complete documents are submitted, evaluation and approval typically take between 24 to 72 hours.
